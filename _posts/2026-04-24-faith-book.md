I used to get upset with my wife on a recurring schedule. Because I had unrealistic expectations. Then one day God changed all that. Comparing my wife's love to God's love led to thinking more about God's love to the point that I was no longer dependent on my wife's love. I still love her and she still loves me but it's better now because I'm more focused on how God loves me.
